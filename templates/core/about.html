A personal reading shelf

Stories, gathered in one place.

MangaVault helps you discover series from supported sources, keep a personal library, follow your reading progress, and prepare chapters as CBZ files.

How it works

Search runs against the sources listed in the app. Reader-capable sources can be imported into a library entry. Catalog-only sources are clearly marked and open on their own site instead. MangaVault does not own or publish the works returned by those providers.

Chapter pages are requested from the selected source when you read or prepare a download. Sources may change, restrict access, or become unavailable, so a result is not a promise that every chapter will load.

Built for your collection

Your library keeps series, chapters, downloaded-file status, and reading progress together. In the current deployment model, authenticated accounts share the catalog. Do not invite people you do not trust until per-account library ownership is implemented.

MangaVault is an independent project and is not affiliated with MangaDex, Comick, MangaUpdates, or other content providers.
