Respect creators and sources

Content and source policy.

MangaVault is a tool for organizing and reading content from third-party sources. It does not own that content or authorize its use.

Source rules

Use only sources that you are allowed to access. MangaVault integrations must use publicly documented interfaces or pages that permit automated access. The app must not defeat paywalls, DRM, logins, CAPTCHA, anti-bot checks, or source rate limits. A source being technically reachable does not mean its content is licensed for every use.

Rights-holder requests

If you believe a source link or item associated with a MangaVault instance infringes your rights, contact the instance operator with the exact title, source URL, relevant material, and an explanation of your rights. The operator should investigate and disable the relevant listing or integration when appropriate. MangaVault does not control upstream provider pages or their hosting.

Attribution and limitations

Search results identify their provider. Metadata-only catalogs open on the provider and cannot be imported as reader sources. MangaVault does not claim affiliation with any listed provider and cannot guarantee that provider data is accurate, authorized, or available.

Instance operators are responsible for choosing integrations, handling complaints, and reviewing applicable law. This policy is a product statement, not a substitute for legal advice.
