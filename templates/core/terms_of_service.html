What the software provides

MangaVault is a self-hostable discovery, library, reading-progress, and chapter-packaging application. It can request data and images from third-party manga sources, but it does not grant rights to material supplied by those sources. Source availability, metadata, and chapter quality are not guaranteed.

Your responsibilities

  • Use sources and download material only when you have the right to access and store it.
  • Follow the terms and rate limits of every source.
  • Do not use MangaVault to bypass paywalls, DRM, account controls, CAPTCHA, or other access restrictions.
  • Protect your login and do not share an account unless the instance operator permits it.
  • Do not attempt to overload providers or the MangaVault instance.

The instance operator manages accounts. Public self-registration is not available. The library is shared between authenticated accounts in the current version. The service may be changed or unavailable when the host, network, or upstream source is unavailable.
